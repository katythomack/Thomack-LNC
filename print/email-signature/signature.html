Add it to your email

Gmail / Google Workspace (on a computer)

  1. Click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it "Thomack LNC".
  3. Click in the box and paste (⌘V on a Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows).
  4. Below the box, set it as the default for new emails and replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom.

Apple Mail (on a Mac)

  1. Open this page in Safari and click Copy signature.
  2. In Mail, go to Mail → Settings → Signatures, pick the account, and click +.
  3. Uncheck Always match my default message font.
  4. Delete the sample text in the right-hand box and paste.

Outlook

  1. Go to Settings → Mail → Compose and reply (or Signatures).
  2. Create a new signature, paste, and save.
