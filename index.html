Services

Merit Screening

Before you commit time and money to a case, I review the medical records to find out whether the care fell below the standard of care and whether that likely contributed to the patient's injury. You get a clear, honest assessment of whether the case has merit.

Records Analysis

Medical records can run to thousands of pages. I organize and review them, build a clear timeline of events, point out key entries and inconsistencies, and identify any records that are missing.

Standards of Care Evaluation

I compare the care the patient received against accepted nursing and medical standards, and I explain in plain language where it met those standards and where it fell short.
